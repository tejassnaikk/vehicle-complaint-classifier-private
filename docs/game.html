Beat the machine

Ten real complaints, one from each system. Pick the system you think failed, then see what our three models chose.
Each game draws 10 of 200 test complaints, one per system, from the masked text in the published data. The complaints are 25 to 120 words long. The models never saw them in training.

What the score means

Ten complaints are a small sample, so one game says little about how good anyone is. On the full time-based test set of 7,320 complaints, the MLP sorts 87 in 100 correctly, the BiLSTM 87 and DistilBERT 89. The Results page has the full scores and intervals.

Some complaints can fairly go in two systems. NHTSA staff filed each one in a single system, and that filing is what counts as the right answer here. If you disagree with it, you are not alone: 513 of 7,320 test complaints were sorted differently from NHTSA's filing by all three models.
