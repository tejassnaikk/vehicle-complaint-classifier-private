Three largest mix-ups

  • 61 complaints about body and frame were put under windshield, glass and wipers.
  • 52 complaints about fuel system were put under engine and cooling.
  • 40 complaints about fuel system were put under electrical system.

Systems that share symptoms are easy to confuse. An engine problem can show up as a fuel problem, and a broken sunroof can count as body or as glass. The Conclusion says more about why. The row and column names are the plain-English names of the ten groups described on the Data & Prep page.

About the examples

The complaints shown are the masked text from the published test data. Each cell shows a random pick of up to five. The percentage is how sure the model was of its answer, so a mix-up with a high percentage is a confident mistake.
