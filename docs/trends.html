How to read these charts

  • Counts depend on who files. A make with more cars on the road gets more complaints, so a long bar is not the same as a worse car. We do not know how many vehicles each make sold.
  • Recent months can look low. Complaints keep arriving after a failure, and the owner may report months later. We make no claim about trends in the last few months.
  • Systems are the ten groups described on the Data & Prep page. A complaint that NHTSA filed under more than one group is not in these counts.
  • Model years before 2010, and complaints with no model year, are grouped together. The make filter lists the 30 makes with the most complaints and one group for all other makes.
